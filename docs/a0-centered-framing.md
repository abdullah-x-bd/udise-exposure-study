# A0-centred research framing

## Substantive focus

The study investigates whether the school system exposes Muslim students in India to patterns consistent with structural disenfranchisement, structural disadvantage and systematically poorer educational conditions.

A0, Muslim enrolment, is the substantive population of interest.

B0, General, C0, Scheduled Caste, D0, Scheduled Tribe, and E0, Other Backward Class, are comparison baselines. They are used to establish whether the conditions associated with rising Muslim concentration are unusually adverse, comparable to other forms of social disadvantage, or explained by broader school composition and geography.

The study does not treat all five groups as equal research subjects. Every principal table, figure and model must foreground A0 and use the other groups as benchmarks.

## Primary questions

1. What school conditions do Muslim students experience nationally in 2024-25?
2. Do school conditions worsen as the Muslim share of enrolment rises?
3. Is the A0 gradient worse than the corresponding B0, C0, D0 and E0 gradients?
4. Are Muslim students more exposed than the baseline groups to schools with restricted class access, teacher scarcity, crowding and weak resources?
5. Do these differences remain after comparing schools within states and districts and accounting for location, management, class span and school size?
6. Is the pattern broad across multiple domains or concentrated in only one indicator?

## Baseline principle

All non-A0 comparisons are analytical baselines.

The baseline groups are not included to produce a general five-group social census. They allow the study to answer whether Muslim schooling conditions are unusually poor relative to advantaged and historically disadvantaged reference populations.

The order of substantive interpretation is:

1. Describe the national condition and exposure of Muslim students.
2. Compare A0 with B0, the General baseline.
3. Compare A0 with C0, D0 and E0 to locate Muslim disadvantage relative to other structurally disadvantaged populations.
4. Use B0-C0, B0-D0 and B0-E0 only to calibrate the size and meaning of the A0 gaps.

## Interpretation

The analysis tests for evidence consistent with structural disenfranchisement and disadvantage. It does not assume the conclusion before examining the data.

Evidence will be considered stronger when A0 disadvantage appears in student-weighted exposure, concentration gradients, within-state and within-district comparisons, adjusted models and multiple independent school-condition domains.

The baseline groups remain essential for identification and interpretation, but all substantive conclusions must return to the social and schooling condition of Muslims in India.
