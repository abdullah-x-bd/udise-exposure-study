# Research plan

## Central question

At the national level, how do the schooling conditions experienced by Muslim students change as Muslim enrolment concentration rises, and how do those gradients compare with schools where General, SC, ST and OBC enrolment concentration rises?

The complete design is specified in `docs/core-national-analysis.md`.

## Focal groups

- A0: Muslim
- B0: General, defined as total minus SC minus ST minus OBC after reconciliation
- C0: Scheduled Caste
- D0: Scheduled Tribe
- E0: Other Backward Class

A0 is a religion category. B0 to E0 are caste categories. These are separate aggregate dimensions and are not five mutually exclusive populations. The source does not reveal the individual-level caste composition of Muslim students.

## Primary comparisons

1. A0 versus B0
2. A0 versus C0
3. A0 versus D0
4. A0 versus E0
5. B0 versus C0
6. B0 versus D0
7. B0 versus E0

The analysis studies the complete concentration gradient rather than relying only on majority-school classifications.

## Unit of analysis

The principal analytical unit is one school in 2024-25. Three distinct estimands will be reported:

1. equal-weighted school-condition gradients
2. total-student-weighted conditions
3. group-student-weighted exposure measures

## Core outputs

1. Reconciled group enrolment by school, class and gender.
2. A0, B0, C0, D0 and E0 school shares.
3. Fixed share bands, national deciles, within-state ranks and flexible continuous gradients.
4. School access and class-span conditions.
5. Building, classroom, water, sanitation, electricity and learning-environment conditions.
6. Digital facilities and devices.
7. Teacher availability, composition, qualification and training.
8. Governance, inspections, grants and school-management conditions.
9. BPL, EWS, repeater, CWSN and age-grade-distortion school-environment measures.
10. Pairwise A0-X0 surfaces and interaction models.
11. Pairwise B0-X0 caste-composition models.
12. National exposure tables and multidimensional deprivation indices.
13. Adjusted school-level models with state, district, management, rural or urban status, school category, class span and enrolment controls.

## Interpretation rules

B0 is General. It is not non-Muslim, Hindu or a residual religion category.

The denominator for A0 will use the reconciled social-category total because the listed religion items do not contain a Hindu category.

BPL, EWS, repeater, CWSN and age-wise enrolment are not cross-tabulated with religion or caste. They can describe the school environment to which a group is exposed, not the group-specific individual rate.

Cross-sectional changes in representation from primary to higher-secondary levels will not be labelled dropout or transition rates.

The study can identify unequal exposure and school-level structural patterns. It will not treat descriptive associations as proof of individual discrimination or causal effects.
