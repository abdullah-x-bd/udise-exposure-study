# Research plan

## Unit of analysis

The principal analytical unit is one school in 2024-25. Student-weighted exposure measures use group-specific enrolment as weights.

## Core outputs

1. School master table joining profile, management, facility and teacher information.
2. Long-form enrolment tables by social category, religion, class and gender.
3. School social-composition measures and majority, plurality and concentration-band classifications.
4. School access measures, including whether the school reaches Classes 10 and 12.
5. Basic infrastructure, sanitation, digital, inclusion, teacher and governance indicators.
6. Student-weighted exposure estimates for Muslim, Christian, Sikh, Buddhist, Jain, Parsi, religion-residual, General, SC, ST, OBC, NR and all students.
7. State, district and block atlases.
8. School-level models with geography, management, school category and enrolment controls.
9. Muslim and OBC, Muslim and SC, and Muslim and ST interaction analyses at school level.

## Interpretation rules

Caste and religion are separate aggregate dimensions in the source. Their school-level overlap can be analysed, but the data do not identify individual Muslim OBC, Muslim SC or Muslim ST students.

NR is defined for analysis as General plus OBC, or equivalently the caste total minus SC minus ST, subject to reconciliation of the source totals. It is not a religion category.

The source schema does not list Hindu enrolment directly. Any residual religion measure will remain labelled `religion_residual` unless the official DCF documentation supports a more specific interpretation.

The study can identify unequal exposure and school-level structural patterns. It will not treat descriptive associations as proof of individual discrimination or causal effects.
