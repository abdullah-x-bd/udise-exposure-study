# CSG policy-deepening studies

This folder contains four linked studies designed to answer a narrower policy question than the original threshold audit:

> When a school's enrolment implies a nominal Composite School Grant entitlement, does the administrative system translate that entitlement into a timely, complete, and durable school-level financial record, and where does any divergence enter?

The studies deliberately separate formula design, eligibility churn, fiscal-record dynamics, and administrative approval. They retain all State/UT observations. Small-sample State/UT cells are flagged rather than dropped.

## Shared definitions

Primary school universe: State/UT and local-government management codes 1, 2, 3, 6, 89, and 90.

Nominal bands used for the UDISE longitudinal studies:
- 1-30: Rs 10,000, retained only as the historical schedule used in the prior audit and always flagged as historically uncertain.
- 31-100: Rs 25,000.
- 101-250: Rs 50,000.
- 251-1000: Rs 75,000.
- >1000: Rs 100,000.

The all-State PAB mechanism audit does **not** assume that the 1-30 amount is historically stable. It reconstructs the band amounts printed in each PAB costing sheet and tests alternative UDISE formula schedules where possible.

Correctly aligned entitlement/report cycles for the fiscal-spell studies:
- 2019-20 entitlement -> 2022-23 UDISE report
- 2020-21 -> 2023-24
- 2021-22 -> 2024-25
- 2022-23 -> 2025-26

This alignment is an empirical administrative mapping, not a claim that money literally takes three years to arrive.

State labels are canonicalized only for obvious spelling/format aliases. Genuine historical territorial changes remain distinct.

## Study 1: cumulative catch-up audit

### Question
When a continuously entitled school records less than its nominal entitlement in one aligned cycle, does the shortfall disappear in later cycles or persist cumulatively?

### Primary estimands
For every continuous same-entitlement spell:
- cumulative nominal entitlement through spell age n
- cumulative recorded receipt through n
- cumulative receipt/entitlement ratio
- cumulative positive gap = max(cumulative entitlement - cumulative receipt, 0)
- share of complete-reporting spells whose cumulative receipt has caught up by n

For every fiscal fallback event, defined as `Meets -> Below` under unchanged entitlement:
- initial recorded deficit
- cumulative catch-up by 1 and 2 subsequent aligned cycles when follow-up exists
- full catch-up, partial catch-up, persistent cumulative gap
- same quantities under missing-as-zero sensitivity

### Guardrails
- A change in entitlement ends the spell. It is never counted as fiscal fallback.
- Missing receipt is unknown in the primary complete-reporting analysis, not silently zero.
- Missing-as-zero is reported only as a sensitivity analysis.
- Catch-up is an administrative-record concept, not independently verified bank receipt.

## Study 2: threshold churn and formula-smoothing counterfactuals

### Question
How often do schools repeatedly move across CSG enrolment thresholds, how close to the threshold are those movements, and how much nominal grant volatility is mechanically generated by the discontinuous formula?

### Primary descriptive outputs
For every school and adjacent year:
- actual band transition
- up-crossing/down-crossing boundary
- distance from boundary
- repeated band changes
- A-B-A "ping-pong" transitions within three observations
- re-entry to the same higher band after a down-crossing within 1, 2, and 3 years

State and national outputs preserve all cells and include sample-size flags.

### Counterfactuals
1. Current annual point-in-time schedule.
2. Two-year average enrolment schedule.
3. One-year hold-harmless: a downward band change is delayed for one year.
4. Near-threshold hold-harmless variants for down-crossings within 5, 10, and 20 pupils of the relevant boundary.

For each rule:
- number of band changes
- repeated-switch count
- absolute nominal entitlement volatility
- aggregate nominal entitlement
- incremental fiscal cost relative to the current rule
- share of actual down-crossings prevented/delayed

The 30/31 boundary remains in the output but is explicitly marked `historical_rule_caution=1`.

## Study 3: fiscal consequence audit

### Question
When recorded receipt falls below an unchanged entitlement, does recorded expenditure also deteriorate, and does expenditure recover when receipt recovers?

### Transition states
Within continuous same-entitlement spells:
- Meets -> Meets
- Meets -> Below (fiscal fallback)
- Below -> Meets (recovery)
- Below -> Below

### Outcomes
- expenditure/entitlement ratio in the destination cycle
- change in expenditure/entitlement ratio
- positive expenditure shortfall relative to entitlement
- cumulative expenditure/entitlement over available follow-up
- receipt-expenditure co-movement within stable spells

Primary contrasts are computed within State x entitlement band x aligned cycle before national pooling:
- fallback minus stable-meet
- recovery minus persistent-below

These are descriptive fiscal consequences, not causal effects of grant receipt.

## Study 4: all-State PAB mechanism audit

### Question
At what point does the observed formula-to-record divergence first become visible: formula construction, PAB approval, or later school-level recording?

### Official source
Department of School Education & Literacy PAB minutes and PRABANDH costing sheets linked from the official PAB archive.

The crawler records every State/UT row. It never drops an unresolved document silently.

### Extraction
For each State/UT and usable financial year:
- official PAB node/page
- PDF document URL
- every `Total of Composite School Grant` occurrence
- recommended DoSE&L amount in lakhs
- component-level band rows where parseable
- parser confidence and unresolved reason

If multiple CSG totals appear in one primary costing sheet, they are summed across elementary/secondary sections after duplicate-line protection.

### Independent alignment test
For every PAB financial year, compare the PAB-recommended CSG total with formula-implied State totals generated from candidate UDISE enrolment vintages and alternative historical small-school schedules.

The audit reports:
- best-fitting enrolment-year lag by State-year and nationally
- absolute and percentage PAB/formula gap
- PAB approval versus later aggregate UDISE recorded receipt/expenditure
- whether divergence appears before or after PAB approval
- unresolved cases, with no imputation

This is intended to test, not assume, the previously reconstructed assignment-to-report timing.

## Six pre-launch validation gates

1. **Boundary gate**: synthetic schools at 30/31, 100/101, 250/251, and 1000/1001 map to the intended bands.
2. **Spell gate**: a changed entitlement ends a fiscal spell; it cannot be labelled fiscal fallback.
3. **Catch-up gate**: a fallback followed by an over-target receipt is classified as cumulative catch-up, while a persistent deficit is not.
4. **Churn gate**: synthetic A-B-A paths are detected, and counterfactual rules reduce the expected switches without changing unrelated paths.
5. **State gate**: obvious aliases merge while genuine historical territorial entities are retained.
6. **Accounting gate**: national totals equal the sum of State/UT outputs within floating tolerance; no State/UT is silently discarded.

The workflow runs these gates plus Python compilation before any expensive UDISE or PAB job starts.
